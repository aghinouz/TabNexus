# TabNexus

## Turn a pile of tabs into a traceable browsing trail.

Once your browser has grown to dozens, hundreds, or even thousands of tabs, the real problem is no longer simply “too many tabs.”

It becomes:

> Where did this page come from?
> What path did I take to find it?
> Will I still be able to find it again days later among hundreds of other pages?

Your browser's tab bar tells you **what you have open**.
Your browser history tells you **what you have visited**.
**TabNexus focuses on how you got from one page to another.**

**TabNexus** turns scattered tabs into an **understandable, searchable, savable, and traceable browsing workspace**, helping you recover the path behind your research and exploration.

## 🌲 1. Understand the Trail: See How Your Pages Are Connected

Real-world web exploration is rarely a straight line. It grows through searches, jumps, branches, comparisons, and detours. TabNexus is designed to preserve that process.

* **Browsing Relationship Tree**: Automatically tracks parent-child relationships between tabs. Instead of seeing isolated webpages, you can see the trail of your exploration as a clear structure. Specific branches can also be extracted or exported directly.
* **Tab-Level Browsing Trail**: Every tab keeps track of where it came from. Long-press the browser's Back/Forward controls in the top-left corner to reveal that tab's dedicated navigation trail, without digging through the massive global browsing history.

## 🔍 2. Find Anything Fast: Precisely Locate Information Across Hundreds of Pages

Once you've accumulated hundreds of tabs, you need something more powerful than scanning them one by one.

* **Automatic Keyword Extraction**: No manual organization required. TabNexus automatically extracts keywords to help you understand what your collection of pages is about.
* **Regex & Multi-Dimensional Search**: Perform advanced searches across page titles and URLs using regular expressions, then instantly reorder results by most recent access time.

## 💾 3. Preserve & Take Control: Turn Browsing Into a Workflow

Your browsing work should not be trapped inside your current browser window. TabNexus provides a complete set of tools for managing complex browsing sessions.

* **Structured Import & Export**: Export matching tabs, including their parent-child relationships, as structured JSON backups. When importing, TabNexus loads tabs incrementally in the background to avoid sudden resource spikes caused by creating large numbers of tabs at once.
* **Restricted Page Protection**: Some local files (`file:///`) and internal browser pages cannot be opened directly due to browser security restrictions. TabNexus automatically creates secure placeholder pages marked **[Restricted]**, ensuring that these pages are not silently lost from your browsing structure.
* **Standalone Window & Batch Actions**: Detach TabNexus into an independent management window, making it especially useful for dual-screen setups. Quickly locate tabs, pin them, copy URLs, or clean up all unpinned items with batch actions.

## ⭐ 4. Native Compatibility: A More Seamless Browsing Experience

TabNexus works with the browser instead of fighting against it.

* **Seamless Tab Groups Integration**: Deeply integrates with native browser Tab Groups. A minimal, arrow-free collapse interaction keeps the interface clean, while collapsed and expanded states stay synchronized with the browser's native Tab Groups in real time.
* **Small Details Matter**: In Firefox, subtle gradient backgrounds make different Containers visually distinct, making container boundaries easier to recognize at a glance.

## 👥 Built for Complex Browsing

TabNexus is designed for people who regularly work with dozens or hundreds of tabs, especially when their workflow looks something like:

> Search → Explore → Compare → Reference → Branch out → Search again → Retrace

It is particularly useful for:

* **Developers**: Move between documentation, GitHub repositories, issues, and codebases while preserving the trail behind your investigation.
* **Researchers & Students**: Keep large collections of references and trace where papers, sources, and supporting information came from.
* **Content Creators & Curators**: Collect large amounts of material and still be able to recover the right source weeks later.
* **Heavy Information Seekers**: People who repeatedly open dozens or even hundreds of pages while investigating a single question.

## 🛡️ Local-First. Your Data Stays Yours.

TabNexus follows a **Local-First** design philosophy:

* **Privacy-Focused**: Your data stays in your browser by default. Browsing records are not uploaded to remote servers.
* **Built for Scale**: Core algorithms are optimized for workflows involving hundreds or even thousands of tabs, keeping navigation responsive and fluid.
* **Native Experience**: Automatically adapts to both Chinese and English interfaces, as well as system light and dark modes.

## **TabNexus** — more than another tab manager.

It is a browsing workspace designed to bring structure to the fragments of information you leave behind while exploring the web.
